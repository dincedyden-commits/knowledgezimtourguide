General Information

The content on this website, including destination guides, itineraries, and travel guide articles, is provided for general informational purposes to help travelers plan a trip to Zimbabwe. While we make every effort to keep this information accurate and current, travel conditions, prices, regulations, and availability can change without notice.

Travel and Activity Risk

Travel to Zimbabwe, including safari activities, wildlife viewing, hiking, and adventure activities such as white-water rafting or bungee jumping, carries inherent risks. Travelers participate in such activities at their own risk and are strongly encouraged to hold travel insurance that covers their specific planned activities.

Wildlife Encounters

Wildlife sightings, behavior, and proximity cannot be guaranteed or controlled, and all wildlife should be treated as potentially dangerous. Travelers must follow guide instructions at all times during game drives, walking safaris, and any wildlife-adjacent activity.
