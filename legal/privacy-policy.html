Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Zimbabwe Tour Guide ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information when you use this website, submit an enquiry, or book a tour with us. By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry or booking form, we collect the information you provide directly, which may include your name, email address, WhatsApp or phone number, travel dates, number of travelers, destinations of interest, and any details you share about your trip preferences.

We do not collect payment card information through this website. Any payment processing for confirmed bookings is handled through separate, secure channels communicated directly with you.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, prepare and send trip itineraries and quotes, communicate with you about a confirmed booking, and improve the trips and information we offer. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share necessary details (such as your name and travel dates) with accommodation providers, transport operators, or other tour partners directly involved in delivering your booked trip.

Cookies and Analytics

This website may use standard analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site, which helps us improve content and navigation. These tools typically collect anonymized or aggregated data such as page views and general location, rather than personally identifying information.

Data Retention

We retain enquiry and booking information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, deliver a booked trip, and comply with any applicable record-keeping obligations.

Your Rights

You may ask us at any time to confirm what personal information we hold about you, to correct inaccurate information, or to request that we delete information that is no longer needed for the purposes described above. Contact us using the details on our Contact page to make such a request.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

Questions about this policy? Contact us and we'll clarify anything that isn't clear.
